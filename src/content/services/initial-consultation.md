## What happens

We will ask what has brought you here and listen to how you describe it, in your own words and at your own pace. You do not need to prepare anything, and you do not need to know what you want from support.

Towards the end, we will share our honest view of what might help. That could be a course of talking therapy with us, a more structured assessment, a different kind of service, or nothing further for now. Whatever the suggestion, you decide what happens next.

## What you can ask us

Anything that would help you decide. People often ask about:

- how therapy sessions work and how many they might need
- the practitioner's training, registration and approach
- confidentiality, and the limits of it
- fees, cancellations and how to stop if it is not working

## Before you book

We will send you a short privacy notice and our terms before the appointment. We do not ask for your medical history through the website. Anything relevant is discussed in the session itself, where it can be handled properly.

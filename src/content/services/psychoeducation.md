## What you will find

Our [resources](/resources/) cover how support is organised in the UK, what different kinds of therapy involve, and what to expect from an assessment. Each article shows when it was last reviewed and lists its sources, so you can check them yourself.

## How we write

We write plainly and avoid overstating what any approach can do. Where research supports a claim, we cite it. Where evidence is limited or mixed, we say so.

## A note on limits

Reading can help you understand what is going on, but it cannot replace a conversation with someone who knows your circumstances. If something you read raises concerns, talk to your GP or [contact us](/contact/).

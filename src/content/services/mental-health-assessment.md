## What an assessment is for

An assessment helps make sense of what you are going through, so that decisions about support are based on a careful understanding rather than guesswork. It looks at the difficulties you describe, how long they have been around, what makes them better or worse, and what else is going on in your life.

It is not a test you can pass or fail, and it does not reduce you to a label. Its value is in the clarity it gives you and anyone you choose to share it with.

## Sharing the outcome

With your agreement, we can send the summary to your GP. We will not share it with anyone else without your permission, except in the rare circumstances described in our [privacy notice](/privacy/).

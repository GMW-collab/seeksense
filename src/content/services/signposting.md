## Why this matters

Sometimes the most useful thing we can do is point you somewhere else. Many good services exist across the NHS, charities and community organisations, but they can be hard to find and harder to tell apart.

We will be honest when we think another service is likely to suit you better than we can.

## What we can help with

- Explaining the difference between NHS Talking Therapies, GP-led routes and specialist teams
- Identifying charities and community groups relevant to your situation
- Thinking through what to ask when you contact a service
- Understanding how to check a private practitioner's registration
